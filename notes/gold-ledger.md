## Sensitivity

- Silk sells at the worst price (20 each): the sale drops 3,360 gp. The lowest balance before line 39 is still 7,280. The end balance is about 2,403.
- Air runes needed (no staff of air): -1,600. End balance about 4,163 at best-case silk.
- Both of the above: end balance about 803.
- Unchecked items (cake sale, beers, Faisal wine, starting gp, shop price rise on 400+ mind runes) are not in these numbers.

## Findings

1. The route is funded without the Eclipse reds on the figures above. The reds would add up to 19,600 gp, which is surplus on these numbers.
2. Line 16 says "Silk trader". The wiki says the Silk trader is in Al Kharid and does not buy silk. The Ardougne buyer is the Silk merchant in East Ardougne.
3. 60 gp per silk is the best bargain price. The guide does not mention haggling.
