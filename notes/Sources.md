## Author-tested (not on the wiki)

- Brutus: the feed-trough safespot takes no damage and needs no dodging (Visit 1 combat table).
- Count Check teleport to Barbarian Village still works, tested 6 November 2026. Not tested after a death or reset.
- Hunter's Guild: Guildmaster Apatura's building and the Eclipse red are usable without Hunter 46.
- Varrock has combat dummies on the castle roof, and the tea stall can be stolen from.
