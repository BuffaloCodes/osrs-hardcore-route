## Author-tested (not on the wiki)

- Brutus: the feed-trough safespot takes no damage and needs no dodging (Visit 1 and Visit 6 combat tables).
- Bull sharks: a safespot on the shore north of the Karamja volcano lets you mage them (Visit 5). The wiki does not mention it.
- Count Check teleport to Barbarian Village still works, tested 6 November 2026. Not tested after a death or reset.
- Hunter's Guild: Guildmaster Apatura's building and the Eclipse red are usable without Hunter 46.
- Veos in Port Sarim takes you to Port Piscarilius for free (author recalls this, not yet tested; Visit 4).
