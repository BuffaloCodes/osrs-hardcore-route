## Author-tested or confirmed (not on the wiki)

- Brutus: the feed-trough safespot takes no damage and needs no dodging.
- Count Check teleport to Barbarian Village works as of 6 November 2026. Not tested after a death or reset.
- Hunters' Guild: Guildmaster Apatura's building and the Eclipse red are usable without Hunter 46.
- Veos in Port Sarim takes you to Port Piscarilius for free (confirmed).
- The Rimmington boat to Ardougne (confirmed).
