# Visit 2 wiki verification (5 November 2026)

Checked against the OSRS Wiki by three fetch agents. Nothing in the guide was changed. Items marked "not found" were not confirmed either way. Step numbers refer to `chapters/Step0_Hardening_v0.03.md`.

## Resolved by in-game testing (6 November 2026)

1. **Count Check (`2.01`).** The wiki says that since 3 Nov 2026 he no longer offers the Barbarian Village teleport on main game worlds. In-game, he still teleports you there. Open: whether he does it again after a death or reset, since the wiki describes it as one-time. The wiki wins over the game only if this stops working.
2. **Combat dummies (`2.23`).** The wiki found none in Varrock, but you have used them in Varrock before. `2.23` stays as written. The wiki page covers only the player-owned-house dummy.
3. **Hunter's Guild (`2.56`, `2.57`).** You can enter Guildmaster Apatura's building and take the Eclipse red despite the Hunter 46 note. "Eclipse red" is a wine bottle, not a moth. Antonius buys it at 700.
4. **Arrow shafts (`2.36`).** You need to world hop for log spawns. `2.36` now says so. For scale, 2,100 shafts need 140 regular logs (15 per log).

## Other facts

- **Chronicle:** 1,000 charges, no recharge except teleport cards (150 gp at Diango's), no cooldown found.
- **Dark wizards:** variants are levels 7, 11, 20, 22 and 23. The Varrock stone circle has levels 7 and 20. Max hit 2 is confirmed for level 7 only. Your table's 6 is not confirmed.
- **Varrock Easy diary:** feed the dog, browse Thessalia's store, buy a newspaper and chop the dying tree are all Easy tasks. The Thessalia task is "browse", not "trade", so adjust `2.08`.
- **Aubury tea:** confirmed as the Rune Mysteries research-notes step.
- **Teak:** Woodcutting 35 to chop (85 XP). Firemaking 35 to burn (105 XP each). Check you have Woodcutting 35 at `2.50`.
- **Axes:** steel needs Attack 5, mithril Woodcutting 21 and Attack 20, adamant Woodcutting 31 and Attack 30 (the draft assumed 20 and 30). Lunami's sells mithril (520 gp) and adamant (1,280 gp). The stock seen was 2 mithril and 1 adamant.
- **Sunbeam ale (`2.65`):** +1 Agility and +1 Strength, 8 gp, sold at Sunlight's Sanctum in Aldarin. The wiki's Taverley Dungeon page mentions no shortcut that uses it. Its shortcuts need 63, 70, 70 and 80 Agility.
- **Antonia:** 20 gp for Sunset Coast to Aldarin.
- **Aggressive NPCs (`2.58`):** only "aggressive jaguars east of Sunset Coast" was found, with no level or max hit. The Jaguars page was not fetched.
- **Toci's Gem Store (Aldarin):** sells uncut sapphire (25 gp), emerald (50 gp) and ruby (100 gp). The stock seen was only 3, 2 and 1, against your 9, 10 and as many rubies as possible.
- **Mistrock:** steel pickaxe is 500 gp.
- **Mountain guide:** the Varlamore one at Quetzacalli Gorge takes you to Auburnvale free. The page named `Mountain_Guide` is the Kourend one.
- **Quetzal:** Children of the Sun is required to use Regulus Cento.
